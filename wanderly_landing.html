Questions

The honest FAQ.

CHAPTER 03 / 03
When does Wanderly launch?
We're targeting Q3 2026 for closed beta and Q4 2026 for public launch. Wait-list members get the first 200 invites and a permanent discount on Pro.
How is this different from Booking, Hopper, or just using ChatGPT?
Booking is great for hotels alone but doesn't think in trips. Hopper is great for flight prices but doesn't connect to the rest of your day. ChatGPT can suggest a trip but can't book it — and half its recommendations are out of date or hallucinated. Wanderly does all three: trip-centric organization, multi-provider bookings, and an AI that's grounded in real, current inventory.
What does it cost?
Free for up to 3 trips with manual entry. Pro is $9/month or $79/year — unlimited trips, AI planner, price alerts, and group sharing. We earn a small commission from providers when you book through us, so subscription pricing stays sane and you don't pay extra at checkout.
Will my data be safe?
Passwords use passkeys by default — no password to leak. Travel documents are encrypted at rest with per-user keys. Payments go through Stripe (PCI-DSS Level 1) — we never see your card. We're Saudi PDPL and GDPR compliant from day one.
Where is Wanderly built?
In Riyadh. Built for the way GCC travelers actually plan — with Arabic, GCC-friendly payment methods, and an understanding that a good trip is half the joy.

Email hello@wanderly.co. Real humans read every message. We're especially interested in talking to travel content creators and small agencies who'd benefit from a B2B tier.
